This page provides detailed information about the accessibility of the MD-SOAR platform. While efforts are being made to achieve full Title II compliance, we appreciate the adverse impacts that non-accessible platforms and content have on users. Please refer to this site for information about the state of our platform and records.

Platform accessibility

MD-SOAR uses DSpace software as its underlying infrastructure. Information about the accessibility of DSpace can be found on the vendor-supplied Accessibility page. Here, you will find the vendor accessibility statement, a brief overview of their accessibility testing methodology, and links to their most recent VPATs. Furthermore, USMAI documentation of platform accessibility is available on the MD-SOAR system accessibility page on the USMAI Portal.

Per the latest DSpace VPAT - dated November 17, 2025 - DSpace largely conforms to WCAG 2.1 AA standards. While there are a few notable factors that are not fully supported, the VPAT indicates that there are development tickets that aim to address these issues. These issues include:

  • WCAG 2.x, Level A
    • 1.1.1 Non-text Content
    • 1.2.1 Audio-only and Video-only (prerecorded)
    • 1.2.3 Audio description or media alternative (prerecorded)
  • WCAG 2.x, Level AA
      1.2.3 Audio description (prerecorded)

We acknowledge that these outstanding compliance issues may cause difficulty for some users to access content on MD-SOAR. However, we are confident that efforts by the DSpace developers will lead to a timely solution to these issues.

Content accessibility

Content on MD-SOAR, sometimes referred to as bitstreams, includes a variety of formats, including conventional word documents such as PDFs, Word documents, and TXT files, research data, audiovisual materials, digitized archival documents, and more. While we appreciate that providing accessible formats for every document is the expectation for Title II compliance, remediating our legacy content - consisting of over 30,000 individual records - is not achievable given our currently available resources. While we are happy to provide on-demand remediation services for individual records (see Requesting record remediation for more information), we are currently working to identify a manageable workflow for remediating our legacy content. More information on this effort will be made available in the future.

Please note that the materials held on MD-SOAR are maintained for reference, research, and record-keeping purposes only. Use of these materials in classroom settings or for instructional purposes may require material remediation in order to remain compliant with Title II requirements.

Roadmap for addressing accessibility issues

As we continue our efforts towards full compliance with Title II regulations, we have created the following roadmap to lead our efforts.

Short term

  • Add accessibility page to MD-SOAR detailing our current known accessibility issues and a roadmap towards compliance
  • Add a hyperlink to global site footer that links to the accessibility page and a direct link to remediation request instructions
  • Add metadata to all MD-SOAR records that indicate their archival and research purpose
  • Add a pop-up message for new submissions that alert record submitters to the expectation of accessible content on MD-SOAR

Medium term

  • Continued monitoring of DSpace development on known accessibility issues
  • Regular review of accessibility monitoring for MD-SOAR via SiteImprove in order to identify and address global accessibility issues
  • Continued monitoring of available accessibility and remediation tools in order to identify a sustainable, cost-effective methodology for remediating legacy content and integrated remediation of new submissions

Requesting record remediation

If you require remediation of a specific MD-SOAR record, please email mdsoar-help@umd.edu.

In your message, please include the following details:

  • A link to the record such as the URL (e.g. https://mdsoar.org/items/12345678-1234-5678-842e-12345678901234567) or the record's permanent link (e.g. http://hdl.handle.net/11603/12345)
  • A brief description of the accommodation that is required (i.e. an accessible PDF format, audio transcription, etc.)

After receipt of this request, a message will be sent to the responsible institution alerting them to the remediation request. Once available, an accessible copy of the record in question will be forwarded to the requestor and the accessible copy will be added to the publicly-available MD-SOAR record. Please note that due to the varying capacity of our institutions, we cannot provide a concrete timeline for responses to remediation requests.
